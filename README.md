# Special Attack Timers

Displays a circle overlay over your special attack orb, an infobox timer, and a better Surge potion timer.

- **Accounts for special attack resets that happen at Colosseum, Doom of Mokhaiotl**
- **Pauses surge potion cooldown timer appropriately at ToB, Colosseum, and Doom of Mokhaiotl**
- Timer resets whenever spec energy actually increases, preventing drift between the overlay and actual game mechanics due to lag
- Configurable colors for all settings

**Important:** Disable "Show spec. attack regen" in Regeneration Meter and "Surge potion timer" in Timers & Buffs.
